# Code of Conduct

KomaEngine adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in the repository, its issues, discussions,
and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and do not harass,
demean, or threaten anyone. Maintainers may remove content and restrict or ban
participants who do not follow it.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers at
**conduct@komaengine.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and the
maintainers will respond and act as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines describe.
